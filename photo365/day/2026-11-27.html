Friday, November 27, 2026 · Practice shot

As I’ve said before, the point of this project is to find meaning and beauty in each day. Sometimes that’s going to be about finding a little peace in the otherwise chaotic mishmash of the world around us. Some days, you wake up knowing exactly what the day is going to bring. Other days, you have no idea, and by the time you sit down and look back on your day, you wonder how you managed to hit all the stops along the way.

Today was definitely one of the latter.

I got most of my Christmas shopping done, put up Christmas lights on Dad’s porch, and unexpectedly relived a little bit of my childhood by chasing a dog named Coconut around Dad’s housing development.

Coconut is a small, white Yorkie mix who reminds me very much of my sister’s dog from when we were kids, Poxy. Despite being the smallest of our three dogs, Poxy was the ringleader of the pack. She would instigate the digging out from under the fence, and before you knew it, you’d be chasing them around Frontenac, with Ace and Midnight, our golden retriever and black lab, happily trailing along behind her. Coconut is a little slower than they were, but we still spent about an hour trying to get her to stop running away from everybody. It’s funny how something as simple as chasing a little white dog can take you back so many years.

Sometimes your day takes another unexpected turn. You go to visit someone who is your brother in all but blood, only to find that he’s accidentally broken a water main in his yard and needs someone to watch the kids.

It’s only 5, and I’m still a ways from home. But of everything I’ve seen and done today, somehow getting these two to stop running around and beating each other up long enough to sit peacefully together is shaping up to be the highlight of my day.

All it took was a little help from the Kung Fu Panda Holiday Special.

Maybe that’s part of what I’m hoping to discover through this project. In the midst of all the things we plan to do and all the things that happen along the way, sometimes the most meaningful moments are the ones we never saw coming.

Maybe it’s a little early to be posting this. Josh just came back in, the water is working again, and we’re rounding up the kids to head to the square for the Christmas tree lighting. No doubt there will be more opportunities for good pictures before the day is over, but sitting here in this quiet little moment, I think I’ve already found the one that best represents today.
