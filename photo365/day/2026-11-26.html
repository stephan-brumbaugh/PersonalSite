Happy Thanksgiving!

I’ve been thinking about this for a long time. I’ve tried a few times over the years, but I’ve never really stuck with it, mostly because I could never find a good platform that made it easy for me to keep up with it. But here we go again! I’m going to try once more to complete the Photo 365 Challenge.

While this is my first post, the challenge won’t officially begin until January 1. Anything I post between now and December 31 will be practice to help me build the habit of taking photos regularly and get into the groove so I’m not hitting January 1 cold turkey.

The intention is to post a picture every day. It could be a carefully planned shot taken with my high-end DSLR or something I quickly shot from the hip with my phone. Either way, the goal is to post something every day that captures the world I live in and the beauty I see in it. Sometimes a photo will be accompanied by a blog post, sometimes just a caption, and sometimes nothing at all. But hopefully, there will be a picture every day.

I’m doing this for a couple of reasons. First, I want to get better about being intentional with my photography. Second, I want to start paying a little more attention to the world around me and looking for the beauty in it.

I’m posting this on a personal website, and chances are very few people will ever see it, but I’m really doing this for myself more than anything. If you’re reading this and want to come along for the ride, welcome!

Stephan Brumbaugh
